## What to keep from early PUA / dating manuals

Usable after ethical translation:

- **Initiative**: start interactions, make invitations, express interest, and accept outcomes.
- **Frame**: maintain calm self-respect; do not over-explain, plead, or collapse under delayed replies.
- **Social proof**: build a real social life rather than performing status.
- **Preselection/social circle**: meeting through groups is safer and more natural than isolated cold approach.
- **Comfort before escalation**: attraction without trust is fragile; do not skip emotional safety.
- **Clear logistics**: good dates require time/place/activity planning.
- **Rejection resilience**: no chasing after a clear no.
- **Self-improvement**: grooming, fitness, communication, social calibration, and stable life rhythm.

Reject or transform:

- Negging -> replace with light, respectful teasing only if rapport exists.
- Manipulative scarcity -> replace with genuine life priorities and non-neediness.
- False time constraints -> replace with natural boundaries.
- Sexual escalation scripts -> replace with explicit consent and comfort checking.
- “Women are prey/reward” framing -> replace with mutual selection.
- Hot/cold punishment -> replace with consistent standards and proportionate investment.

## Mars/Venus useful principles

Treat gendered claims as heuristics, not rules. Apply as “people may differ in stress and communication styles.”

- Some people respond to stress by withdrawing to solve privately; others regulate by talking.
- When someone vents, ask whether they want listening or problem-solving.
- Relationship trust is built through repeated small responses to connection requests.
- Conflict often escalates when one person offers solutions before acknowledging emotion.
- Appreciation matters; people want their efforts seen.
- Relationship stages include attraction, uncertainty, exclusivity, intimacy, and commitment.
- Long-term relationships require maintenance, not just initial chemistry.

## Adlerian useful principles

Because the PDF text was not OCR-readable, use only broadly known Adlerian concepts:

- Inferiority feelings are normal; overcompensation can appear as arrogance, control, or people-pleasing.
- Stable self-esteem reduces neediness and domination.
- “Striving for superiority” should become constructive competence, contribution, and courage.
- Social interest/community feeling matters: relationships are cooperative, not conquest.
- A person’s lifestyle pattern shows in how they handle rejection, competition, intimacy, and responsibility.

## Core operating model

Attraction and relationship progression require five layers:

1. **Self-state**: grooming, body, energy, study/career direction, emotional stability.
2. **Social surface area**: repeated group exposure to appropriate women.
3. **Communication**: low-pressure opening, listening, humor, self-disclosure, invitation.
4. **Escalation**: from group familiarity to private chat, low-stakes date, explicit interest, relationship definition.
5. **Long-term fit**: values, conflict repair, family, money, sex, life planning, marriage readiness.
